Xbox, PlayStation, Switch

  1. In your console network settings, set primary DNS to 104.238.130.180 and secondary to 8.8.8.8
  2. Open Minecraft and go to Servers
  3. Join any featured server
  4. Pick Connect to a Server
  5. Enter winkreyes.duckdns.org, port 20795

Set your DNS back to automatic later if you want.

If the game feels slow or choppy

  1. Open Settings, then Video. Set Graphics to Classic or Fast (not Fancy or Vibrant).
  2. Find Render Distance and slide it lower, to about 6 to 8.
  3. Close other apps running on your phone, tablet, or computer.

Still slow? Close the game and open it again.

Plugin guide

Plugins are add-ons that give the server extra features. Here is what each one does, in plain words. Tap a name below to read about it.

First, what is a command?

A command is a short message you type in the chat box. It always starts with a slash, like this: /spawn

To open the chat box on a computer, press the T key. On a phone or tablet, tap the speech bubble at the top of the screen. On a console, press right on the D-pad. Type the command and press Enter or Send.

When you see something in pointy brackets, like <name>, swap it for the real thing. So /tpa <name> becomes /tpa Grandma. Don't type the brackets.

How to use an active ability

  1. Reach the required level and hold the right tool.
  2. Use/right-click the tool to ready it. On a phone or controller, use your control scheme's Use / Interact action.
  3. Within 4 seconds, do the matching action: mine, chop, dig, harvest, or hit a target.
  4. Watch for the ability message. After the boost ends, most active abilities have a 4-minute base cooldown. Blast Mining has its own activation and a 1-minute cooldown.

The usual level-50 boosts start at about 3 seconds, grow to 12 seconds at level 500, and reach 22 seconds at level 1000. Player perks can change the timing.

Nothing happened?

Check your level with the skill command and your timer with /mccooldown. Make sure you're using the right tool and target. With something in your off-hand, try sneaking while you ready the tool. /mcability toggles ability activation, so check its message instead of repeatedly switching it.

Touch controls differ by Minecraft control scheme. A long press is not the same action on every phone.

Keep family builds safe. Tree Feller can take down connected logs, and Blast Mining explodes immediately. Practice far from homes, animals, and other players. Repair may remove enchantments; Salvage destroys the item.

What the names and arrows mean

  • North is up. Your name and marker are green, with a * beside your name. Other players' names are yellow.
  • Inside the mapped area, arrows show which way a player is facing.
  • At the edge, arrows point from the map's center toward an out-of-area player. This may be a different direction from your own current position.
  • Lines connect labels to their real positions. When players stand together, the labels spread out to help you read them.
  • On a crowded map, some labels take turns being shown. Type /playermap list for the full text list of visible online players and their worlds.

A map of one area

Builder Mode: build big without touching your Survival life

Created by us: ExcelBuildMode. Its in-session editing tools come from WorldEdit (third-party, by EngineHub), installed alongside it; without WorldEdit, build sessions would not have their selection, clipboard and undo tools.

Builder Mode is a construction-only mode for trusted builders. While it is on, you build in Creative with flight. The moment you enter, your Survival life is snapshotted: inventory, armor, offhand, ender chest, XP and hotbar. When you switch back, everything is restored exactly as it was.

Who can use it, and how

  • Marvin (GumGumJetPistol) is the only builder in the pilot, by the server owner's decision. More builders can be added only by the owner.
  • Type /buildmode on to enter a build session, /buildmode off to return to normal play, and /buildmode status to check which mode you are in.
  • Everyone else plays completely normally. Builder Mode changes nothing for players who never enter it.

No earning while building, by design

  • Jobs pays nothing for anything you do during a build session.
  • Combat is off both ways: you cannot hurt anything, and nothing can hurt you.
  • Projectiles, fishing, harvesting and stripping are blocked (composters, sweet berry bushes, beehives, campfires, and axe-stripping logs).
  • You cannot talk to NPCs or open shops during a session.
  • Rewards you already earned in normal play before entering still settle normally.

WorldEdit inside a session

  • Selections, set, replace, walls and faces work, along with clipboard copy, paste, rotate and flip, and undo/redo of your own edits.
  • Server-wide limits apply to everyone: at most 5,000 blocks per operation, a 10-step undo history, and every WorldEdit command is logged.
  • Brushes included (1.2): WorldEdit brush commands (//brush) work in session: the construction brush families, plus the brush options for size, material, range and mask. The butcher brush is deliberately not included; it kills mobs in a radius, which is combat, not construction.

Safety and restore

  • You cannot be hurt during a build session, so there is no dying in Builder Mode.
  • If you log out mid-flight, you rejoin in a protected landing hold (still flying, empty hands), and your Survival items return the moment you land.
  • Restore also runs on normal exit and on the recovery paths after a rejoin or a server restart, so a session cannot strand your Survival inventory.
