How to join

Phone or iPad

Tap the Add server to Minecraft button above. Or do it yourself:

  1. Play, then Servers, then Add Server
  2. Name: Wink Reyes Valley
  3. Address: winkreyes.duckdns.org
  4. Port: 20795

Windows (Bedrock)

  1. Play, then Servers, then Add Server
  2. Name: Wink Reyes Valley
  3. Address: winkreyes.duckdns.org
  4. Port: 20795

Java on PC or Mac

  1. Multiplayer, then Add Server
  2. Address: winkreyes.duckdns.org:20795

Xbox, PlayStation, Switch

  1. In your console network settings, set primary DNS to 104.238.130.180 and secondary to 8.8.8.8
  2. Open Minecraft and go to Servers
  3. Join any featured server
  4. Pick Connect to a Server
  5. Enter winkreyes.duckdns.org, port 20795

Set your DNS back to automatic later if you want.

If the game feels slow or choppy

  1. Open Settings, then Video. Set Graphics to Classic or Fast (not Fancy or Vibrant).
  2. Find Render Distance and slide it lower, to about 6 to 8.
  3. Close other apps running on your phone, tablet, or computer.

Still slow? Close the game and open it again.

Plugin guide

Plugins are add-ons that give the server extra features. Here is what each one does, in plain words. Tap a name below to read about it.

First, what is a command?

A command is a short message you type in the chat box. It always starts with a slash, like this: /spawn

To open the chat box on a computer, press the T key. On a phone or tablet, tap the speech bubble at the top of the screen. On a console, press right on the D-pad. Type the command and press Enter or Send.

When you see something in pointy brackets, like <name>, swap it for the real thing. So /tpa <name> becomes /tpa Grandma. Don't type the brackets.

mcMMO: what will you unlock next?

Mine, fish, farm, fight, and get better as you play. Every skill has its own level, and you can work on all of them. You don't have to choose a class.

Some rewards happen automatically. Others are short boosts you switch on. Pick a skill below to see what it does, when it unlocks, and how to use it.

How to use an active ability

  1. Reach the required level and hold the right tool.
  2. Use/right-click the tool to ready it. On a phone or controller, use your control scheme's Use / Interact action.
  3. Within 4 seconds, do the matching action: mine, chop, dig, harvest, or hit a target.
  4. Watch for the ability message. After the boost ends, most active abilities have a 4-minute base cooldown. Blast Mining has its own activation and a 1-minute cooldown.

The usual level-50 boosts start at about 3 seconds, grow to 12 seconds at level 500, and reach 22 seconds at level 1000. Player perks can change the timing.

Nothing happened?

Check your level with the skill command and your timer with /mccooldown. Make sure you're using the right tool and target. With something in your off-hand, try sneaking while you ready the tool. /mcability toggles ability activation, so check its message instead of repeatedly switching it.

Touch controls differ by Minecraft control scheme. A long press is not the same action on every phone.

Keep family builds safe. Tree Feller can take down connected logs, and Blast Mining explodes immediately. Practice far from homes, animals, and other players. Repair may remove enchantments; Salvage destroys the item.

Useful commands

  • /mcstats see all your skill levels
  • /mining see one skill; swap in /fishing, /repair, or another skill name
  • /mccooldown check when your active abilities are ready
  • /mcability turn ability activation on or off
  • /mctop see the skill leaderboard
  • /party info check your party and unlocked features

On Java, the compass menu's mcMMO Stats button also runs /mcstats. You can always use chat if a menu doesn't have that button.

Play together in a party

A party is a little team. Use Minecraft account names in commands, which can be different from display nicknames.

  1. One person types /party create Family (or chooses another team name).
  2. Invite someone with /party invite PlayerName.
  3. The invited player types /party accept.
  4. Check /party info together. Use /party quit to leave.

Party features unlock as the party's level rises: chat at 1, party teleport at 2, alliances at 5, item sharing at 8, and XP sharing at 10. These are party levels, separate from your skill levels. Features also depend on permissions; the compass teleport-request menu is still the easiest way to visit family.

How XP sharing works at party level 10

The party leader can use /party xpshare equal to share, or /party xpshare none to stop. Eligible teammates within 75 blocks share the earned skill XP pool.

With the checked settings, 100 earned XP shared by two nearby teammates becomes a 150-XP pool: 75 each, before individual modifiers. Sharing helps everyone progress together, but does not mean each player gets 150 XP.

Not installed or live yet. These instructions describe the tested mcMMO 2.3.003-WRV-XP-1 and WeekendXP 1.0.2 pair. The bonus indicator and new green-bar rewards will be available only after installation and startup checks.

Together: join the same mcMMO party and stay less than 32 blocks from at least one other online teammate in the same world. Where enabled and permitted, eligible skill XP and green-bar XP receive 1.25×, or 25% more points. Party level 10 is not required for this separate Together bonus.

Read the bonus bar

A separate bonus bar is shown by default and refreshes roughly once per second. ×1.00 means the base bonus factor. If skill XP and green-bar XP have different factors, it shows them separately. Restrictions or unavailable green-bar support are labeled instead of promising a reward.

  • /xpbonus or /xpbonus status shows your current factors, skill-specific rates, and relevant restrictions
  • /xpbonus hide hides your bonus bar without turning off your XP rewards
  • /xpbonus show brings your bonus bar back
  • /xpbonus toggle switches your bar between shown and hidden

Your shown/hidden preference is saved for later joins. If a factor is unexpected, check that your teammate is in the same party and world, is close enough, and that the status command does not show a permission or area restriction.

Which green-bar XP counts?

The bonus applies to eligible, positive XP-orb pickups after Mending. It boosts the points left for your green bar, not Mending repairs. Normal identified gameplay sources include mining, mob drops, fishing, furnace output, breeding, trading, bottles o' enchanting, and grindstones.

Direct command/API and advancement XP grants do not pass through this pickup bonus. Identified player-death refunds and unmarked custom or unknown orbs are excluded. Tiny fractional bonuses carry forward across eligible pickups instead of being lost.

Other plugins and merged XP orbs can affect source identification. This is not a guarantee that every refunded or custom orb can be distinguished. Actual Java/phone display and live gameplay checks are still pending.

Player Map: find family in the world

Preview instructions

This ordinary-map upgrade is not installed on the family server. These instructions describe the prepared prototype. Real phone/Java display checks are still needed. The former BlueMap web map has been removed.

Use an ordinary empty map as usual. The upgrade adds player names and arrows to newly activated maps while you hold them, keeping Minecraft's normal terrain and exploration. No browser, resource pack, or client mod is needed by this design.

Start a mapRead the markersZoom, copy, and frameTroubleshooting

Start a map normally

  1. Get an ordinary empty map and use it to start mapping the area. No map-giving command is needed.
  2. On a phone, hold it in your main hand. Java players can also hold it in their offhand.
  3. Explore normally to fill in the terrain. The added names show eligible online players in the same world, including players beyond the map's terrain coverage.

Maps you already had, explorer maps, and treasure maps are not automatically upgraded. Custom maps are also skipped. The prototype does not give you free maps on join, death, or reconnect. Type /playermap for help.

What the names and arrows mean

  • North is up. Your name and marker are green, with a * beside your name. Other players' names are yellow.
  • Inside the mapped area, arrows show which way a player is facing.
  • At the edge, arrows point from the map's center toward an out-of-area player. This may be a different direction from your own current position.
  • Lines connect labels to their real positions. When players stand together, the labels spread out to help you read them.
  • On a crowded map, some labels take turns being shown. Type /playermap list for the full text list of visible online players and their worlds.

A map of one area

The map keeps its fixed, vanilla-aligned area. It does not follow or continuously recenter on you. Faraway unexplored terrain is not filled in automatically.

Use the normal cartography table

  • Add paper to zoom out. The five normal scales cover 128, 256, 512, 1,024, and 2,048 blocks across. A larger area means less detail.
  • Add an empty map to make a copy. You can share maps with family; they are not owner-locked.
  • Add a glass pane to make a locked copy. That locked map stays plain, without the added live-player overlay. Use locked maps for walls and map art.

Zoomed maps keep Minecraft's normal exploration behavior. A held map and an unlocked framed copy with the same map ID can share rendered imagery, so an unlocked frame is not a separate, reliable live-player display.

Changing dimensions: the map remains a map of its original world. It does not switch to a different map when you travel through a portal. Players in another world are not placed at converted coordinates.

If something looks wrong

  • No added names? Check that this is a newly activated, unlocked ordinary map and hold it in your hand. Existing and special maps keep their normal behavior.
  • A player is missing? They may be offline, in another world, or hidden from the added overlay by permissions or supported vanish settings. Use /playermap list to check. Minecraft's ordinary map cursors still follow its own rules.
  • The map is crowded? Labels cycle about every three seconds at the default update rate. Long or unsupported name characters may be shortened or shown as a question mark.
  • Lost a map? There is no automatic replacement. Keep a normal copy somewhere safe if you want one.

Player positions are sampled about once per second, with extra client/network delay possible. Actual phone readability, Geyser behavior, and live-server performance still need acceptance checks.

The BlueMap browser map was removed on October 12, 2026. There is no longer a live BlueMap web map to open. Your Minecraft worlds and player progress are unchanged by this removal.

A held-map upgrade is being prepared. See the Player Map preview for its status and instructions.

Behind the scenes

These work quietly in the background. You never need to touch them.

  • Geyser, Floodgate, ViaVersion, ViaBackwards: Let phones, tablets, consoles, and computers all play together, even on different game versions.
  • LuckPerms: Decides who is allowed to do what.
  • Vault: Keeps track of everyone's money so the other add-ons agree on it.
  • PlaceholderAPI and CMILib: Helper pieces the other add-ons need.
  • Chunky: Builds the world ahead of time so it loads faster.
