How to join

Phone or iPad

Tap the Add server to Minecraft button above. Or do it yourself:

  1. Play, then Servers, then Add Server
  2. Name: Wink Reyes Valley
  3. Address: winkreyes.duckdns.org
  4. Port: 20795

Windows (Bedrock)

  1. Play, then Servers, then Add Server
  2. Name: Wink Reyes Valley
  3. Address: winkreyes.duckdns.org
  4. Port: 20795

Java on PC or Mac

  1. Multiplayer, then Add Server
  2. Address: winkreyes.duckdns.org:20795

Xbox, PlayStation, Switch

  1. In your console network settings, set primary DNS to 104.238.130.180 and secondary to 8.8.8.8
  2. Open Minecraft and go to Servers
  3. Join any featured server
  4. Pick Connect to a Server
  5. Enter winkreyes.duckdns.org, port 20795

Set your DNS back to automatic later if you want.

If the game feels slow or choppy

  1. Open Settings, then Video. Set Graphics to Classic or Fast (not Fancy or Vibrant).
  2. Find Render Distance and slide it lower, to about 6 to 8.
  3. Close other apps running on your phone, tablet, or computer.

Still slow? Close the game and open it again.

Plugin guide

Plugins are add-ons that give the server extra features. Here is what each one does, in plain words. Tap a name below to read about it.

First, what is a command?

A command is a short message you type in the chat box. It always starts with a slash, like this: /spawn

To open the chat box on a computer, press the T key. On a phone or tablet, tap the speech bubble at the top of the screen. On a console, press right on the D-pad. Type the command and press Enter or Send.

When you see something in pointy brackets, like <name>, swap it for the real thing. So /tpa <name> becomes /tpa Grandma. Don't type the brackets.

mcMMO: skills that get better with practice

What it does: You get better at things by doing them, just like real life. Dig a lot and your digging skill goes up. Chop trees and your tree skill goes up. Each skill has a level, a number that shows how good you are.

What leveling up does: Higher levels unlock little bonuses. For mining, there are three:

  • Double Drops: Sometimes a rock gives you 2 pieces instead of 1. The chance grows slowly. At level 100 it happens about 1 time in 10. At level 1000 it happens every time.
  • Super Breaker: Hold your pickaxe and right-click (or tap and hold) to get ready. Then start mining. For a few seconds you dig super fast and get extra rocks. The higher your level, the longer it lasts.
  • Blast Mining: Unlocks at a higher level. Exploding TNT gives you more ore and less junk.

Playing together: Make a party (a little team) with family. When you play near each other, everyone gets extra skill points. That is the Together bonus.

Weekend bonus: On Saturday and Sunday (Central time) everyone levels up 1.2 times faster.

What you can type:

  • /mcstats see all your skill levels
  • /mining learn about one skill (swap in any skill name, like /woodcutting)
  • /mctop see who has the highest skills
  • /party create <name> start a team
  • /party invite <player> ask someone to join your team
  • /party join <player> join someone's team

Jobs: get paid for everyday things

What it does: Pick a job and you earn pretend money for doing normal things. A Miner gets paid for digging rock. A Farmer gets paid for harvesting crops. It's like a paycheck for playing.

There are 12 jobs: Brewer, Builder, Crafter, Digger, Enchanter, Explorer, Farmer, Fisherman, Hunter, Miner, Weaponsmith, and Woodcutter. You can join as many as you like.

What leveling up does: The more you work a job, the higher its level, and the pay goes up a little (about 1% more each level). Jobs only pay money. They don't give special powers.

What you can type:

  • /jobs browse see all the jobs and tap one to pick it
  • /jobs join <job> join a job, like /jobs join Farmer
  • /jobs leave <job> quit a job
  • /jobs stats see your job levels

Essentials: getting around and money

What it does: Handy shortcuts. Jump back to the starting area, save your house as "home" and pop back to it, visit family, and check your money.

Good to know: There are no free starter items. If you die, you drop everything you were carrying. Sleep in a bed and you'll come back to that bed when you die.

What you can type:

  • /spawn go back to the starting area
  • /sethome save the spot you're standing on as home (you can save up to 3, give each a name like /sethome farm)
  • /home go back home (or /home farm)
  • /delhome <name> forget a saved home
  • /tpa <name> ask to jump over to someone
  • /tpaccept or /tpdeny say yes or no when someone asks to come to you
  • /warps see the list of special places, and /warp <place> to go there
  • /msg <name> <message> send a private note to one person
  • /bal see how much money you have, and /balancetop see the richest players
  • /motd see the welcome message again

GriefPrevention: put a fence around your stuff

What it does: Lets you claim land, like owning a yard. Nobody else can break or take anything there unless you say it's okay.

How to claim: Hold a golden shovel. Right-click (or tap) one corner of your land, then the opposite corner. That's your yard now. You earn more land to claim just by playing.

What you can type:

  • /trust <name> let someone build in your yard
  • /untrust <name> take that permission back
  • /claimslist see all your claimed land
  • /abandonclaim give up the claim you're standing in

CoreProtect: the undo button

What it does: It quietly keeps a record of who placed and broke what. If something gets wrecked, Mike can see who did it and put it back. You don't need to type anything.

EliteMobs: tougher monsters and adventures

What it does: Some monsters are stronger than normal. They have a level number, and harder ones drop better rewards. There are also adventure areas to explore when you're ready for a challenge.

Tip: A higher-level monster is tougher. Bring good gear, or bring family.

Shopkeepers: villager shops

What it does: Shops run by villagers. Walk up, right-click (or tap) the villager, and trade, like buying at a market stand.

Server menu compass

What it does: The compass in your bag opens a menu with big buttons, like Spawn, Set Home, and Home. No typing needed. Just use the compass (right-click or tap) and pick a button.

What you can type: /menu opens the same menu.

Leaderboards: who's on top

What it does: Scoreboards showing who's done the most, including the richest players.

What you can type: /leaderboard see all the boards

No Phantoms

Phantoms are flying monsters that show up if you skip sleeping for a few nights. They're turned off here.

Restart warnings

Sometimes the server takes a quick nap to refresh. You'll see a countdown message first, so you have time to get somewhere safe.

World map

A map of the whole world you can look at in a web browser. Ask Mike for the map link.

Behind the scenes

These work quietly in the background. You never need to touch them.

  • Geyser, Floodgate, ViaVersion, ViaBackwards: Let phones, tablets, consoles, and computers all play together, even on different game versions.
  • LuckPerms: Decides who is allowed to do what.
  • Vault: Keeps track of everyone's money so the other add-ons agree on it.
  • PlaceholderAPI and CMILib: Helper pieces the other add-ons need.
  • Chunky: Builds the world ahead of time so it loads faster.
